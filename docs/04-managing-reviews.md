# Managing Customer Reviews

Reviews (testimonials) are the customer quotes shown on your homepage and About page — the kind of social proof that builds trust with new visitors. This is under the **Review** section of the sidebar.

## Add Review

A simple form to manually enter a customer testimonial:

- **Customer Name**
- **Title** (e.g. their job title, or a short label like "Verified Buyer")
- **Country**
- **Comment** — the actual quote/testimonial text
- **Photo** — an optional photo to display next to the review (if you don't add one, the site automatically shows a neat circular badge with the customer's first initial instead — so a review always looks complete even without a photo)

## Review Manager

Lists all reviews currently in the system. From here you can:
- Edit an existing review's text or details.
- Delete a review.
- Control whether a review is **active** (shown on the public website) or not.

## How This Connects to the Public Website

A review only appears to customers if it's marked active. Once active, it shows up in two places:
- The **homepage** testimonials section.
- The **About page** testimonials section.

Both sections pull from the exact same list — you only enter a review once, and it appears everywhere it's meant to.

## Where Do Reviews Come From?

Right now, there's no self-service way for a customer to submit their own review directly on the website — every review currently comes from your staff typing it in through **Add Review**. If a customer emails or messages you positive feedback, you'd copy it in here manually to have it appear on the site.
