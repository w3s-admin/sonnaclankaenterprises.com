# Understanding Your Website — Start Here

This is a plain-English guide to what your website system actually does. No technical jargon — just what it is, who uses it, and what you can do with it.

## The Big Picture

Your system has **two separate sides**:

1. **The Public Website** — what customers see when they visit `sonnaclankaenterprises.com`. Anyone on the internet can browse it. No login needed.
2. **The Admin Panel** — a private, password-protected back office where you and your staff manage everything: adding vehicles, handling reviews, managing staff accounts. Customers never see this.

Think of it like a car showroom: the **public website** is the showroom floor customers walk through, and the **admin panel** is the office in the back where staff update the inventory, price tags, and paperwork.

## How These Two Sides Connect

Almost everything a customer sees on the public website was put there by someone using the admin panel:

| A customer sees this on the website... | ...because someone did this in the admin panel |
|---|---|
| A vehicle listed for sale, with photos and price | Staff used **Add Vehicle** to enter it |
| A vehicle marked "Sold" and removed from listings | Staff used **Vehicle Manager** to mark it sold |
| A customer testimonial on the homepage or About page | Staff used **Add Review** (or a customer's review was approved) |
| The "X Cars In Stock" number on the homepage | Automatically counted from vehicles currently in the system |
