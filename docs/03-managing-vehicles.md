# Managing Vehicles

This is the part of the admin panel you'll use the most — adding cars to your stock, editing them, and marking them sold. Everything here is under the **Vehicle** section of the sidebar.

## Add Vehicle

This is the form you use to put a new car up for sale on the website. When you submit it, the vehicle appears on the public website almost immediately (in the "New Arrivals" section on the homepage, and in the full stock listing).

The form asks for:

**Basic identification**
- Stock (which stock category it belongs to)
- Category / Type
- Make, Model, and free-text model description
- Body Type
- Chassis Number
- Registration Number

**Specifications**
- Colour
- Engine Capacity
- Transmission
- Fuel Type
- Year & Month
- Mileage

**Pricing**
- Price
- "Highest Offer" — a checkbox for when you want to accept the best offer instead of listing a fixed price
- Special Offer Amount — an optional discounted price to display

**Features / Options** (tick whichever apply to this specific car)
AC, Power Steering (PS), Power Windows (PW), Power Mirrors (PM), ABS, TV, CD, DVD, Alloy Wheels, Air Bag, Rear Camera, Fog Lamp, Sunroof, Leather Seats, Winker Mirror, Rear Wiper, Smart Key, and a free-text "Other Options" field for anything not covered by the checklist.

**Photos**
- Upload one or more images of the vehicle. These become the photo gallery customers see on that vehicle's detail page.

## Vehicle Manager

This is your **inventory browser** — a searchable, filterable list of every vehicle currently in the system (whether sold or not). Use it to:

- **Search/filter** your stock by Stock category, Category, Body Type, Colour, Make, Model, Transmission, Chassis number, a free-text keyword, or a specific reference/ID number.
- **Edit** any vehicle's details (opens the same form as Add Vehicle, pre-filled with its current info).
- **Mark a vehicle as sold** (see below).

This is the page you'll open most often day-to-day — think of it as your master inventory list.

## Marking a Vehicle as Sold

From Vehicle Manager, you can mark any vehicle as sold. This:
- Records the selling price.
- Removes it from the active "for sale" listings customers browse on the website.
- Moves it into your sold-vehicle history.

## Unsold List

A quick, **printable report** of every vehicle still in stock (not yet sold) — showing reference number, make, model, chassis, colour, and its feature flags at a glance. It opens as a pop-up window with a "Print" button, useful for a quick physical inventory sheet or handing to someone who needs a paper list.

## How This Connects to the Public Website

| You do this in the admin panel | The customer sees this on the website |
|---|---|
| Add Vehicle → fill in details → submit | The car appears in "New Arrivals" on the homepage and in the full stock listing, immediately |
| Tick feature checkboxes (AC, Sunroof, etc.) | Those same features show on the vehicle's detail page |
| Upload photos | Those photos appear in that vehicle's gallery |
| Set a Price | That exact price displays on the listing and detail page |
| Mark a vehicle as Sold | It disappears from the "for sale" listings entirely |
| Edit any field | The website updates to match, immediately — no delay, no separate "publish" step |

There is no approval step or delay — whatever you enter in the admin panel is what customers see on the live site right away.
