# The Public Website — What Customers Can Do

This is everything a visitor can do on `sonnaclankaenterprises.com` without logging in. No account needed — anyone who finds your site can do all of this.

## 1. The Homepage

The first page a visitor lands on. It's built to give a quick, confident first impression and guide people toward browsing your stock. It contains, top to bottom:

- **A large welcome banner** with your logo, a headline, and a real, live count of how many cars you currently have in stock, plus a spotlighted vehicle.
- **A quick search box** — visitors can immediately filter by Make, Model, and Body Type without leaving the homepage, then jump straight to matching results.
- **"New Arrivals"** — a showcase of your most recently added vehicles, with photo, price, and key specs (transmission, year, mileage) shown right on the card.
- **"Why Choose Us"** — four short trust-building points (full inspection, direct sourcing, worldwide shipping, real support).
- **A call-to-action banner** inviting visitors to get in touch or browse the full stock.
- **Customer testimonials** — real reviews you've approved (see [Managing Customer Reviews](04-managing-reviews.md)).
- **The footer** — company contact details, quick links, a newsletter sign-up box, and your social media link.

## 2. Browsing Vehicles ("Our Listing" / Stock)

Visitors can browse your **entire vehicle inventory**, with filters to narrow down:

- Filter by Make, Model, Body Type, Transmission, and other specs.
- Search by keyword or by a specific vehicle reference number.
- Each result shows a photo, price, and quick specs at a glance.
- Clicking a vehicle opens its full details page (see below).

There's also a **"New Vehicles"** version of this same browsing experience, focused specifically on your newest stock.

## 3. Viewing a Single Vehicle

When a visitor clicks into a specific car, they see:

- A full photo gallery of that vehicle.
- Complete specifications: make, model, year, mileage, engine size, transmission, fuel type, color, body type, and any extra features/options.
- The price.
- A **"Call Now"** button and a **WhatsApp** button, so they can contact you directly about that specific vehicle without filling out any form.
- A location map showing your office.

## 4. The About Page

Tells visitors who you are: your company history (established 2003), what you do, and key numbers (vehicles supplied, years in business). Also shows customer testimonials again, reinforcing trust.

## 5. The Contact Page

- A contact form — when a visitor fills this in and submits, it's **emailed directly to your business email** (`info@sonnaclankaenterprises.com`). You don't need to log into anything to receive these — they just arrive in your inbox like a normal email.
- Your address, phone numbers, and business hours, clearly listed.
- An embedded map showing exactly where your office is.

## 6. The WhatsApp Chat Button

A floating button that stays visible on every page. Clicking it opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to your business number, so visitors can start a conversation instantly from their phone.

## 7. Newsletter Sign-Up

In the footer, visitors can type their email address to subscribe to updates. This adds their email to a subscriber list stored in the system (see [Newsletter & Email Tools](06-newsletter-emails.md) for what happens with that list on the admin side).

## What Visitors *Can't* Do

- They can't buy a vehicle directly on the site (no online checkout) — every path leads to contacting you by phone, WhatsApp, or the contact form.
- They can't create an account or log in as a customer — there's no customer login system. The only login on this site is for your staff (the admin panel).
- They can't post a review directly on the site — reviews are entered by your staff in the admin panel (possibly based on feedback customers send you separately).
